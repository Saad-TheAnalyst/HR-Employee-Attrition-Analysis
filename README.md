# # HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of IBM HR Employee Attrition data using Excel 
Pivot Tables and Dashboard to uncover why employees 
leave the company and identify key attrition patterns.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts
- Excel Dashboard

## Questions Answered
- How many employees left vs stayed?
- Which department has the highest attrition?
- Which age group leaves the most?
- Does business travel affect attrition?
- Which job role has the highest attrition?

## Key Findings
- Overall attrition rate was 16.1% with 237 out of 
  1470 employees leaving the company.

- Sales department had the highest attrition rate 
  at 20.6% followed by Human Resources at 19%.

- Young employees aged 18-21 had the highest 
  attrition rate showing that younger staff 
  are more likely to leave.


- Employees who travel frequently had the highest 
  attrition rate at 24.9% compared to 8% for 
  employees who do not travel at all.

- Sales Representatives had the highest attrition 
  rate at 39.8% while Research Directors had 
  the lowest at just 2.5%.

## Dataset Source
Kaggle - IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
